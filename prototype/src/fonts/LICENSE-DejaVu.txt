DejaVu Sans, version 2.37 (subset)
Source: https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/

Files in this folder:
  DejaVuSans-subset.ttf        DejaVu Sans Book, 378 glyphs
  DejaVuSans-Bold-subset.ttf   DejaVu Sans Bold, 358 glyphs

The files are subsets of the original fonts. They keep 332 characters: Basic Latin,
Latin-1 Supplement, Latin Extended-A (with the Polish letters), dashes, quotation marks,
bullet, ellipsis, right arrow and the "almost equal" sign. All other glyphs were removed;
the remaining glyphs and the font names are unchanged.
